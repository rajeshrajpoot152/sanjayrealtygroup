Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about international land acquisition, clear titles, and our global services.

Can foreigners buy agricultural land in India?

Generally, non-resident Indians (NRIs) and foreign citizens cannot directly purchase agricultural land in India. However, there are specific exemptions and legal structures available for commercial developments and inheritance. Our legal team handles the complete due diligence and structuring to ensure compliance.

What is the typical timeline for an international land transaction?

An international land transaction typically takes 60 to 90 days from the initial agreement to final registration. This timeline includes comprehensive legal checks, title verification, escrow setup, and government approvals. We ensure a seamless end-to-end process.

How do you ensure the land has a clear title?

We conduct rigorous legal due diligence spanning the last 30 to 50 years of ownership history. Our expert legal partners verify local land records, check for encumbrances or litigations, and secure title insurance where applicable to guarantee 100% clear ownership.

Do you help with property management after the purchase?

Yes, our services extend beyond the transaction. We offer comprehensive property management solutions, including leasing, maintenance, and tenant management, ensuring your investment remains hassle-free and profitable.

What are the tax implications of buying international property?

Tax implications vary significantly depending on the country of purchase and your residency status. We collaborate with expert international tax consultants to provide you with tailored advice, ensuring you understand all potential tax liabilities and benefits.
